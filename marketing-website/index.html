01 / Why Storybound?

Learning can feel like magic.

Reading and spelling can take extra effort for children with dyslexia. Long written instructions and one-size-fits-all activities may leave some children needing more time, repetition or support.

Parents may also have questions about dyslexia and want a clearer view of how their child is doing during practice at home.

✦
Our idea

Storybound uses picture cards, meaningful visual cues and spoken guidance to make reading and spelling activities more accessible and engaging. Through three story-led mini-games, children can practise selected English literacy skills at their own pace, while parents gain a clearer view of their progress.

Storybound is intended as a supplementary learning resource, not a dyslexia diagnosis or a replacement for specialist teaching.

09 / Questions & answers

Curious about the library?

Here are a few things parents and teachers may want to know.

Still curious? Get in touch ↗
Who is Storybound designed for?

The game is being designed for children aged 6–8 with dyslexia, focusing initially on selected English literacy skills.

Can I play Storybound on this website?

No. This is the marketing website. Storybound itself is planned as a downloadable 2D educational game, with desktop and laptop computers prioritised for the initial version.

What makes the story rewards different?

After completing a level, a child can earn stars, choose a magical character and unlock a short, narrated picture story linked to what they practised.

Will parents be able to see progress?

The planned parent dashboard will summarise activity recorded inside Storybound. It does not measure all school learning or provide clinical assessments.

Does the AI chatbot diagnose dyslexia?

No. The proposed parent-only chatbot will provide general information and learning guidance. It will not diagnose dyslexia or replace qualified professionals.

Is the game available to download now?

Storybound is currently in development. Adults can register interest to hear about development updates when the website's form is activated.
