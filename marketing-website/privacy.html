Your privacy matters

Privacy notice

Status: This notice is a launch-ready template but must be reviewed and completed with the team's actual contact details, hosting arrangements and data-retention policy before collecting real emails.

What the interest form collects

When an adult submits the optional registration form, the website asks for an email address, their selected role (parent/caregiver, teacher or other adult) and confirmation that they agree to receive updates. It does not ask for children's names, ages, dyslexia diagnoses or other sensitive information.

How information is used

The team intends to use these submissions only to share relevant Storybound development updates or respond to expressed interest. The information must not be used for unrelated purposes without a suitable legal basis.

Who processes submissions

If deployed following the included setup guide, the interest form is processed through Netlify Forms and stored in the hosting account associated with the project. The team must confirm the actual service providers, retention period, authorised access and any applicable transfer arrangements before making the form live.

Mini-game and chatbot demos

The three mini-games and parent chatbot preview run in the browser. Game answers are not sent to a server or saved as progress records. The chatbot preview uses prewritten, keyword-selected responses; it is not connected to a live AI model. Do not enter a child’s personal or sensitive information.

Fonts and technical requests

This website loads fonts using Google Fonts. Standard hosting providers may also collect essential technical access logs. No optional analytics, advertising cookies or tracking scripts are included in this website package by default.

Your choices and contact

Participation in the interest form is optional. People should be able to contact the project team to ask questions, withdraw from updates or request a review of their submitted information. Before going live, add the verified team contact email and specify the retention and deletion procedure here.

Do not submit children's personal data through the marketing website.
